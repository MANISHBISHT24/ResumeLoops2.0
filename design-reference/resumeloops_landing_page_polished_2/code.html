Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS and why does ResumeLoops optimize for it?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software programs used by companies to collect, sort, and scan resumes. If your resume lacks proper formatting or keywords, it gets filtered out before a human ever sees it. ResumeLoops ensures 100% compatibility.

Can I export my resume to PDF and Word formats?

Yes! You can instantly export your resume as a clean, highly formatted PDF or a Microsoft Word document with a single click.

How does the AI bullet point rewriter work?

Our AI analyzes your experience level and job description to suggest action-oriented, metric-backed bullet points that highlight your professional achievements effectively.
