## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet luxury through an editorial, architectural lens. Designed for ambitious professionals and executives crafting career documents, the experience prioritizes restraint, deliberate hierarchy, and tactile calm over aggressive SaaS mechanics. The emotional register is authoritative, serene, and confident—evoking the weight of high-grade stationery, archival curation, and bespoke publishing.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Editorial Craft**:
- **Purity of Structure**: Structural order governed by crisp 1-pixel rules, deliberate column divisions, and unhurried whitespace.
- **Architectural Typography**: Dramatic optical scale contrasts utilizing pure neo-grotesque type—balancing oversized tracking-tight headlines against fine tabular metadata.
- **Simulated Paper Realism**: Live document canvases render with true-to-life paper proportions, subtle warm shifts, and tactile elevation rather than synthetic neon gradients or decorative illustrations.
- **Understated Intelligence**: AI features manifest as precise, discreet assistance tinted in slate-stone neutrals rather than fluorescent tech accents.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in archival neutrals, unbleached cotton papers, and deep lithographic blacks. 

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF9F5`)**: Natural linen ground for the master viewport.
- **Warm Surface (`#F4F3EF`)**: Secondary contextual background used in toolbars, sidebars, and structural wells.
- **Soft Panel (`#F0EFEB`)**: Embedded inspection trays, muted chips, and nested document panels.
- **Elevated Card (`#FFFDF8`)**: High-white rag paper finish reserved for real-time document sheets and floating modals.

### Typography & Structure
- **Primary Ink (`#1B1B1B`)**: High-contrast, deep neutral black for titles, primary body copy, and focused states.
- **Secondary Ink (`#77746E`)**: Warm umber-tinted neutral for secondary descriptions, section labels, and descriptive body copy.
- **Muted Metadata (`#A29E95`)**: Low-contrast pigment for timestamps, character tallies, keyboard shortcuts, and disabled states.
- **Subtle Divider (`#D9D7D0`)**: Architectural hairline borders and structural rules.

### Action & Functional Accents
- **Primary Action (`#181818` / Text: `#FFFFFF`)**: Obsidian solid for high-conviction decisions, primary CTA buttons, and active toggle markers.
- **AI Intelligence Accent (`#6B6B65`)**: Mineral graphite tone used for AI prompts, generative suggestions, and smart auto-formatting indicators.
- **Success (`#426B50`)**: Deep spruce green for ATS readiness passes and verified exports.
- **Warning (`#9B7338`)**: Dry ochre for formatting warnings and length alerts.
- **Error (`#BA3D32`)**: Brick carmine for syntax and validation interruptions.

## Typography

The typographic expression uses **Inter** across all roles to achieve an architectural, editorial rhythm through proportion and tracking:

- **Large Scale Display (`display-xl`, `headline-lg`)**: Set at lighter weights (400–500) paired with tight negative tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.025em`). This prevents oversized headers from feeling commercial, producing a modern broadsheet appearance.
- **Document Body Copy (`body-lg`, `body-md`)**: Relaxed line-heights (1.6x) ensure dense professional chronologies remain breathable and effortlessly readable on both digital monitors and printed mockups.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`label-md`, `label-xs`)**: Tuned with slight positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.05em`) and uppercase variants where appropriate, reinforcing crisp cartographic precision for word counts, ATS scores, and metadata badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical split workspace: an ambient fixed-width configuration rail paired with a broad fluid stage for document composition.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Canvas Margins**: Set generously at `margin` (48px) on desktop to create a frame of calm around active documents. Compresses to `margin-mobile` (20px) on mobile viewports.
- **Workspace Split**: 
  - **Desk & Panel View (Desktop >= 1200px)**: A 380px fixed-width left rail for content input, AI refinement, and resume modules, adjacent to an expansive fluid preview canvas centered with standard letter/A4 aspect ratios.
  - **Single Pane (Mobile < 768px)**: Segmented tabs toggle between structural edit mode and document preview, maximizing vertical focus.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed strictly by multiples of 4px and 8px. Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled inputs and chip contents; reserve `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px) for separating document sections, work experience entries, and container margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system abandons heavy drop shadows in favor of **tonal surface stacking** and **tactile ambient diffusion**:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved horizontally and vertically through shifting values: `#FAF9F5` base canvas → `#F4F3EF` control rail → `#F0EFEB` input troughs.
- **1-Pixel Architectural Rules**: Interfaces rely on `1px solid #D9D7D0` border lines rather than shadow edges to define panels, toolbars, and modal bounds.
- **Paper Canvas Shadow**: The central live document sheet uses an ultra-fine double shadow simulating heavy-weight 120gsm unprinted cotton rag:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(27, 27, 27, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 27, 27, 0.05)`
- **Modals & Overlays**: Floating menus and command palettes lift off the canvas with a crisp border and an ambient low-opacity diffuse plume:
  - `0 0 0 1px #D9D7D0, 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(27, 27, 27, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is compact and tailored, matching the formal language of paper products and technical stationery.

- **Base Radii (`4px` to `6px`)**: Standard buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and status tags feature sharp, refined corners (`roundedness: 1`).
- **Containers & Document Sheets (`8px` to `12px`)**: Application panes, live resume canvases, and floating drawers utilize controlled `8px` or `12px` boundaries.
- **Pills**: Disallowed for primary interactive controls. Pill treatments are strictly reserved for status pips and ATS verification indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid obsidian `#181818` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius, 40px height. Hover brings a subtle lift to `#2C2C2C`. Active state settles at `#000000`. No shadow.
- **Secondary (Outline)**: 1px border in `#D9D7D0`, background `#FAF9F5`, text `#1B1B1B`. Hover transitions background to `#F4F3EF` with border `#1B1B1B`.
- **Ghost / AI Prompt**: Background transparent, text `#6B6B65`, hover background `#F0EFEB` with text `#1B1B1B`.

### Input Fields & Text Areas
- Single-line inputs and narrative blocks feature `#F4F3EF` fill with an understated `1px solid #D9D7D0` outline and `4px` radius.
- **Focus State**: Background shifts to `#FFFDF8` (Elevated Card tone), border changes to `#1B1B1B` with zero glow ring.
- Placeholder text in `#A29E95`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Metadata Tag**: `#F0EFEB` surface, `#77746E` text, `4px` corner radius, typography set to `label-xs` in uppercase tracking.
- **AI Suggestion Chip**: Hairline outline `1px solid #D9D7D0` with `#FFFDF8` background. An unobtrusive `#6B6B65` spark icon leads the label.

### Lists & Chronology Items
- Experience and education items render with structural line separators (`1px solid #D9D7D0`).
- No card-in-card wrapping. Reordering handles use minimalistic `#A29E95` twin-hairline grips that darken to `#1B1B1B` on hover.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square forms (`checkbox`) feature `2px` corners; circular selectors (`radios`) use a nested dot.
- Unselected: `1px solid #D9D7D0` over `#FFFDF8`.
- Selected: Solid `#181818` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` internal marks.

### Document Sheet Preview
- Represents the resume page in `#FFFDF8` with the ambient paper shadow.
- Text selection inside the canvas renders in an archival tone: `background: #E8E5DD; color: #1B1B1B;`.
- Floating contextual formatting menu appears above text selection with a crisp `1px solid #D9D7D0` contour and `#FFFDF8` background.
